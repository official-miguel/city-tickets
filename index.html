    How it works

    From search to gate in minutes

    Gates usually open one to two hours before kick-off. Show the code on your e-ticket on your phone, or bring a printed copy, and stewards will scan you in.

    1. Choose a match Browse by sport or search for your team.
    2. Pick your seats Select a stand and the number of tickets.
    3. Pay securely Pay by M-Pesa or card to confirm your order.
    4. Walk in Scan your e-ticket at the gate. That is all.

    Help

    Frequently asked questions

    How many tickets can I buy per order?

    Up to 8 tickets per match, per order.

    How will I receive my ticket?

    An e-ticket with a unique code is shown on your confirmation page and sent to your email immediately after payment.

    Can I get a refund?

    If a match is cancelled you are entitled to a full refund. If it is postponed your ticket stays valid for the new date. Other refunds depend on the organiser's policy shown on the event.

    Who sets the ticket prices?

    The team or organiser of each match sets its prices and ticket categories. Talanta City Sports handles the selling and delivery.

    How do I sell tickets for my team?

    Email or call us using the details in the Sell tickets section. We will verify your organisation and list your matches.
